3. HANDWRITTEN GEOMETRIC ARGUMENT: WHAT CLOSES AND WHAT DOES NOT

Fix one compact regular pure-power source P, one target horizon/width, one
actual full joint tuple, its older interpreting homomorphism, and its retained
bank values. Maintain the prefix-frozen and atomic-reference invariants.

Induct on k, the number of free coordinates.

Base k=0:
All coordinates are fixed. The slice is empty or a singleton. This is a
terminal validity/sign test, not a recursive connectivity oracle.

Base k=1:
Use the ordered real roots of the actual univariate fiber equation. Distinct
root labels give the finite point pieces. Existing native univariate slice
and root/gap machinery is the terminal computational kernel.

Step k>=2:
Choose the deterministic next free coordinate h and a permitted auxiliary
coordinate g. Select the valid nonzero exceptional factors under the SAME
older homomorphism. Cut the h-axis at:
  (a) their real bad-factor roots; and
  (b) ACTUAL real projection-critical values.
Properness gives a bounded range. The sets are finite with degree-derived
bounds. Outside these cuts, a singular fiber would produce an actual critical
value, a contradiction. Existing chart/section machinery then transports
components across each open slab.

At an endpoint of type (a), extend by the corresponding correlated scalar cut.
At an endpoint of type (b), use its real critical-point witness and extend by
the full physical critical command. Advance freezes h, preserves the original
source under prefix freezing, shifts references and decreases k. Apply the
induction hypothesis to the endpoint slice.

A genuine physical point over an interior midpoint selects a continuous
physical branch. Its ordered-coordinate word has fixed length (n+1)*B.
The existing Near theorem identifies the branch endpoint at a cut. Connect the
branch node to the lower-rank endpoint-component node exactly when that endpoint
belongs to the named child component. This is the missing computational meaning
of the selectors: Near identifies a point, not its recursive component.

Form a graph of slab branches and endpoint-child nodes. Each accepted edge is
an actual path by branch continuity or child induction. Conversely, use the
finite accessible slab cover: any path induces a walk through incident pieces.
These statements establish reachability iff JoinedIn.
Existing FiniteSlabBitCover.connected_computable
(FiniteSlabBitAlgorithms.lean:59) performs finite graph
search once its local-cover and exact touching-fiber hypotheses (:30-51) are
instantiated.

This is a sound on-paper induction conditional on explicit local descriptor,
attachment and record-coherence obligations. An existential finite-cover
theorem does NOT supply those obligations.

The PRESERVED FullDescending.exists_full_recursive_cover:104 and
FullRegularCover.regular_zero_full_cover_original:53 return only 'there exists
a finite I with a ClopenPathCover'. They repair real endpoint witnesses but
erase the descriptor/trace relation needed by the algorithm.

4. REAL CRITICAL VALUES: THE KNOWN PITFALL AND A CONCRETE REPAIR

The implication
  real root of an elimination polynomial -> real joint critical point
is false. This is not a Lean type-alignment issue.

An example within the proper pure-power form is
  P(x,y,z)=x^4+y^4+z^4+4*y^2-6*z^2+1.
For a real x-projection critical point,
  dP/dy=4*y*(y^2+2)=0 forces y=0;
  dP/dz=4*z*(z^2-3)=0 forces z=0 or z^2=3.
The real zero equation then gives only x^4=8; the z=0 case would require
x^4=-1. Thus actual real critical values are +/-8^(1/4).
However the complex solution y^2=-2,z=0 gives P=x^4-3 and a REAL projected
coordinate +/-3^(1/4). These are ghost elimination values. The interval
(1,3/2) contains the positive ghost but no actual real critical value.
The zero set is smooth: every real full-gradient zero has P=1 or P=-8.
Properness follows from its positive quartic leading part.
This is an on-paper critical-elimination example, not a newly run computation
of a particular repository linePolynomial.

Consequently, excluding all height-annihilator roots suffices for regularity
but can destroy completeness if every cut endpoint must have a physical
critical witness. Excluding the four selected bad-factor roots is a different
condition: those roots are deliberately scalar cuts.

A plausible native repair, worth attempting before changing the command ABI:
- Extend the fixed parent by one actual h-critical command.
- Scan complete words for that FULL joint system, not independent coordinate
  roots and not just its nonsingular solutions.
- Require agreement with ALL retained old bank quotient values.
- Test whether the new selected h-coordinate lies strictly between the cuts.
- Reject the slab exactly if such a word exists.

Soundness follows from full critical-row readback. For completeness, an actual
real critical point extends the same parent tuple/hom and receives a complete
word. The sampler already captures singular roots as well:
SquareSampleComplete.Complete/root_complete:29/37,
SquareSampleCompleteVertices.word_valid/candidate_word:51/61, and
IntegralFramedPhysicalSquare.complete:84.

Remaining proof duties for this proposed scan:
agreement of all old quotient values must determine old generator images and
hence the same hom; the extra task must have honest cap/source/native bounds;
every geometric witness must produce an accepted word.
Multiple critical points can share a height. Count/order distinct HEIGHTS,
for example by filtering coordinate-root ordinals with this witness predicate,
rather than counting joint critical-point witnesses as distinct cuts.

An alternative is to support scalar-only ghost-height cuts explicitly.
That requires a new/extended record contract and cost proof; the physical
critical tag cannot silently acquire that meaning. It is a design alternative,
not a proved shortcut.

5. BOUNDED ENCODING ARGUMENT AND ITS MISSING INVARIANT

There are two different label bounds:
- The ORIGINAL auxiliary-candidate/tag graph already has uniform polynomial-bit
  widths and native validity.
- The INTERNAL recursive roadmap still needs a whole-record/descriptor bound.
The first does not prove the second.

At a freely supplied external radius, let L=N+bitlength(R), including any
already bounded query labels. The bounded algorithm should be polynomial in L.
An original-input bound follows after R is supplied by an original-only
BinaryFn writer. Do not silently identify these two input sizes.

Available cardinal quantities:
  U=(3*(n+1)+1)*2^B               bounds cut values per stage;
  M=2^(B*(n+1))                  bounds physical branch codes;
  C_k <= (U+1)*(M+2*C_(k-1))       at recursive ranks k>=2.
Old DescendingCard.coverFactor:22 gives C_k <= coverFactor(n,B)^k.
RealCutCard.sweepBadReal_ncard:22 preserves the cut bound after the repair.
Hence log2 C_k is polynomial when k,n,B have the required original bounds.

Use the additive node layout 'branch OR left-child OR right-child', as the
existing selected-index construction does. Do not store the whole recursion
tree or an exponentially long graph walk.

The encoding proof must introduce one explicit invariant:
  every visited descriptor names the same original source and an honestly
  embedded saved bank, and all records fit a common fixed target cap.
If one frame needs at most c new records and q(L) local bits, prove, for total
record length ell,
  ell(0) <= q0(L),
  ell(k) <= ell(k-1)+q(L),
  records(k) <= records(0)+c*k
(or the correct explicit recurrence if this optimistic form is false).
With k polynomial in L, these are polynomial in the augmented input length.
Then compose an original-only bounded radius writer to obtain the final bound.
Charge every numeric field, full physical column and terminal complete-word
width. 'Depth is polynomial' alone does not establish these formulas.

The most exposed local obligation is sibling/midpoint coherence:
a and b may be roots chosen under an earlier parent hom. After adjoining a,
the decoder's CURRENT factor/current-Q can differ. Prove exact transport for
adjoining b, or give a supported old-parent construction. Then derive the
literal scale-2/refsum midpoint constraint 2*y_h=a+b in the saved bank.
Separate realizations of each endpoint under different homs are insufficient.

For each encoded node prove:
  valid word -> actual geometric piece/point and stated ancestry;
  required geometric node/attachment -> bounded valid word;
  native local edge/touch test iff the claimed attachment.
Choosing a finite injection into bitstrings is not a decoder or a space bound.
Local Encoded/Correlated realization does not establish these global properties.

6. GLOBAL STABILIZATION: A CONDITIONAL PROOF USING EXISTING LEMMAS

A stronger whole-family isotopy theorem is NOT necessary for closed-source
SAME-component queries among fixed finitely many sample anchors.

Let X be the original closed source and A=(a_i) its fixed finite good samples.
They do not change with outer radius. Let r_marks bound their norms by
2^r_marks. CandidateRadius and AuxiliaryCandidateOuterRadius already give
sample bounds/persistence; they do not bound paths between samples.

BoundedPathExhaustion.finite_marked_radius:37 gives SOME M such that all
pairwise JoinedIn answers among A are preserved in X intersect closedBall(0,M).
Proof: choose a path for each joined pair, bound each compact path image, then
take a maximum over finitely many pairs. The converse is inclusion.
This is already formalized, and is qualitative.

Let H_ij(R) be the answer of a CORRECT bounded roadmap at dyadic radius 2^R,
transferred through existing bounded marked regularization.
BoundedMarkedComponents.marked_component_iff:100 and
BoundedRegularComponents.component_iff_lift:170 supply that fixed-radius transfer.

Now assume this MISSING uniform-presentation theorem:
there are integer parameter polynomials p_alpha(T), a bound b depending only
on original input, and R-independent Boolean dependence of all H_ij(R)
on their signs at T=2^R, for every R>=r_marks; furthermore
  coefficientMass(p_alpha) <= 2^b for EVERY alpha,
and b has a uniform native polynomial-length binary code (BinaryFn b).

The hypothesis must cover ALL recursive primitives, side conditions, chosen
root/branch identifications, endpoint tests and coherent labels. Fixed-radius
PSPACE alone does not imply it. Bounds that grow with the R being bounded
do not give the needed noncircular conclusion.
An older homomorphism and selected roots generally vary with T; freezing them
while changing radius is invalid. The integer outer-sign lemma does not apply
directly to arbitrary real/algebraic coefficient families. Include denominator
nonvanishing, degree drops, zero branches and root/ordinal selection. A graph
schema whose width changes with R needs a coherence theorem, or an independent
uniform presentation of its final marked-pair answers.

Then set
  R_star=max(r_marks,b+1).
IntegerPolynomialOuterSign.family_stable:59 stabilizes all those signs, and
hence all H_ij, for R>=R_star.
Choose R_big>=R_star with 2^R_big>=M. At R_big, finite-path exhaustion and
bounded roadmap correctness give the ORIGINAL answers.
Constancy transports exactly those answers back to R_star.
The reverse path implication also follows immediately by inclusion.
Binary control of r_marks and b makes R_star computable with polynomial bit
length; compose the native bounded backend with that radius writer.

This conditional argument is complete on paper. Numerical cutoff algebra is
already proved. The genuinely missing theorem is the uniform sign presentation
with original-only mass bound, plus the correct bounded roadmap.

Existing BoundedRegularRadiusPolynomial.polynomial_eval:53,
BoundedRegularRadiusBounds.polynomial_degree:69/parameterWidth_polynomial:99,
and BoundedRegularRadiusMass.coefficient_bound:138 polynomialize and bound ONE
regular source in T=2^R. They do not yet bound every recursive eliminant,
primitive comparison and branch decision. Generic quantifier elimination may
lose the required bounds; it is not an automatic substitute.

Review the uniform statement BEFORE more parameter-reader implementation.
If it cannot be proved with the needed bounds, consider a symbolic
parameter-at-infinity route instead; that also needs correctness/space proofs
and is not a completed alternative.

A sample-radius shortcut is invalid. On the parabola
  y=M*(1-x^2),
the points (-1,0),(1,0) lie in the unit ball, but every joining path crosses
(0,M). Bounding representative coordinates cannot bound their joining paths.

7. FROM CLOSED-SOURCE CONNECTIVITY TO ORIGINAL BOOLEAN FRONTIERS

This is a separate obligation, not an automatic final wrapper.

For actual original pieces P_a,P_b inside S, the required edge is
  exists x in P_a intersect closure_S(P_b),
  OR the symmetric condition.
It is NOT merely 'weak sign conditions are compatible', and not just
'some closed-source candidates are in the same component'.

The semantic canonical covers transport closed-source components through
proper projection, saturation, circular retraction and radial access.
Prove exact incidence/attachment transport for THESE operations, or construct
a different effective accessible cover with two-way graph correctness.

An explicit frontier lemma must account for:
- a boundary point permitted by the ORIGINAL Boolean formula;
- the named component on both sides, not merely their sign patterns;
- approach from that component, witnessed by a coherent limiting branch;
- endpoint-singleton/base incidence as well as base/base incidence.

All closures must be relative to S. For S=R minus {0}, negative and positive
pieces have ambient closures meeting at 0 but are disconnected in S.
Using that excluded point as an edge is wrong.

Qualitative accessibility and graph equivalence are already proved, but
FormulaSignBoundary.boundary_envelope gives only a necessary weak-sign
condition. A native iff for component-indexed relative closure is missing.
Writing 'connectedComponent' or 'closure' in a predicate and invoking ETR
is not a reduction: those predicates must first have polynomial-size/native
algebraic witnesses. Real quantification is not free bounded BIT quantification.

A closed-source roadmap algorithm should expose a boundary-access/limit query
contract usable by this stage. Finishing SAME-component membership alone
does not close the arbitrary Boolean target.

8. PAPER CHECK AND CHOICE OF DIRECTION

The supplied Berkeley report's Theorem 3.5, pages 14-15, imports the roadmap
algorithm from [C87b]. It replaces algebraic-number approximation and sign
operations by PSPACE sign machinery and bounds the remaining enumeration and
graph search. Those pages are not a self-contained geometric proof of
roadmap coverage, encoding and global truncation.
The ETR theorem earlier in that report is distinct and is already present in
Abstract.etr_mem_PSPACE; it is not the missing connectivity theorem.

The local supplement tmp/pdfs/canny-general-roadmaps.pdf is the LATER
'Computing Roadmaps of General Semi-Algebraic Sets', not [C87b].
Its pages 7-8 discuss component preservation and a stratified deformation
argument; page 8 also discusses a large outer parameter and small inner
parameter via leading/lowest terms. This is useful context, not a theorem
already instantiated here. Do not mix the papers or count that later argument
as an existing local library bridge.

No simpler unconditional ETR shortcut was found. Polynomial-size existential
formulas for local feasibility help. A formula containing semantic component
membership, or an explicit exponentially long roadmap walk, does not establish
the desired reduction. Existing native one-dimensional slice connectivity is
a base case, not arbitrary-dimensional adjacency.

Recommended direction: finish a SINGLE effective bounded-regular roadmap
contract before growing more arithmetic backends. Keep it as a milestone,
not a silent weakening of the arbitrary Boolean/unbounded final targets.
