The adaptation uses the project's existing Lean and Mathlib pins rather than adding
a TauCeti dependency. All adapted declarations live in `LeanCanny.Algebra.Sturm` or
`LeanCanny.Algebra.PrincipalSubresultant`, so they are included in the project axiom audit. Changes include:

- Keep the list-variation recurrence and scaling law, the finite jump-sum lemma,
  and only the second-entry lookup from the upstream sequence extensions.
- Keep the constant-sign and simple-root-crossing lemmas. Prove constant signs
  over ℝ using Mathlib's intermediate value theorem, avoiding the abstract
  real-closed-field development and the one-sided endpoint API.
- Specialize the analytic chain and Tarski theorems to ℝ. The signed remainder
  identities and the removal of common factors still work over ordered fields.
- Prove the whole-line theorem directly from the finite-interval theorem by
  choosing endpoints beyond every chain root. Retain the leading-coefficient
  and degree-parity formulas at infinity; omit the half-line formulas.
- Rename helper declarations into the project namespace and adapt imports and
  proofs to the pinned Mathlib.

[`SturmQuery.lean`](LeanCanny/Algebra/SturmQuery.lean) is the project bridge:
coefficient maps preserve signed remainder sequences and their sign variations,
rational-input queries agree with the sum of signs at distinct real roots, and
these formulas substitute into the scaled sign-pattern count.

The signed-principal-coefficient slice additionally keeps the Euclidean reduction
and degree-drop identities, the zero-gap permanences-minus-variations statistic,
and their correctness formula. It omits reducta/specialization results, keeps
only the needed corner-minor Laplace expansion and binomial identity, and avoids
an analytic Cauchy-index dependency. `Sturm.remainderIndex` is defined directly
by coefficient variations; its recurrence and the subresultant formula work
over ordered fields.

The local permanences-minus-variations module also exposes the zero-gap weight
and a continuation recurrence. The project uses this API to prove the streaming
accumulator in `SignStatistic.lean` and its encoded space bound in
`SignStatisticSpace.lean`; these two modules are new project proofs.

[`PrincipalSubresultant/Minor.lean`](LeanCanny/Algebra/PrincipalSubresultant/Minor.lean)
is the project's convention bridge: each principal matrix equals the existing
Sylvester minor, including terminal indices, and the signed integer coefficients
inherit its bit budget. [`PrincipalQuery.lean`](LeanCanny/Algebra/PrincipalQuery.lean)
connects the determinant formula to real-root Tarski queries and sign-pattern
counts, with rational and integer input specializations.

These are kernel-checked mathematical correctness results. The reference uses
Mathlib's noncomputable polynomial operations. The project modules
`PrincipalCoefficientSpace.lean` and `PrincipalCoefficientQuery.lean` supply an
encoded principal-sign pipeline from polynomial coefficient readers and FP degree
metadata. Retaining computed degrees and composing this pipeline with the
original head/query sources remains open.
